Privacy

EveryGame Offline™ is a free collection of games: Rarebit, Wild Trumps, Shed, Cheat, Wordl, Memory, 2048 and Backgammon. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your scores, streaks, settings, saved games and the nickname you type are stored only on your own phone or browser. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data, removes them.

Online rooms

When you create or join a room in Shed or Cheat, your device connects directly to the other players' devices using PeerJS. A PeerJS server helps the devices find each other. Only the game itself and the nickname you chose are sent to other players in your room. Nothing is kept after the room closes.

Other services

  • Fonts load from Google Fonts.
  • Rarebit may load answer photos from Wikipedia.

These services receive your device's internet address when they send files, as any website does.

Children

The games are suitable for all ages and collect no personal information from anyone.
